# Wanderlust Chronicles — Travel Blog & Interactive World Atlas

A self-contained editorial travel blog website showcasing travel stories, an interactive world destination map, photo gallery slideshows, reader comments, social sharing, an About the Traveler page, and a Travel Resources toolkit.

## Features Implemented
1. **Homepage**: Featured cover story, brand header & navigation, interactive world map, and recent articles feed.
2. **Blog Articles**: Full narrative dispatches with headings, captions, field-tested travel tips, and video dispatch highlights.
3. **Travel Destinations & Filtering**: Filter articles by region (`Asia`, `Europe`, `South America`) and category (`Destinations`, `Travel Guides`, `Food Experiences`, `Cultural Insights`), plus a dedicated Destinations directory.
4. **Interactive Map**: Clickable destination pins plotted on the world atlas that open the corresponding travel article directly.
5. **Photo Galleries & Lightbox Slideshow**: Each article includes a multi-photo gallery with a full-screen lightbox viewer and previous/next slideshow controls.
6. **User Comments & Social Sharing**: Interactive comment form with `localStorage` persistence and social media sharing buttons.
7. **About the Traveler & Resources**: Dedicated traveler biography with travel statistics, upcoming expeditions, interactive packing checklist, and booking guides.

## How to Run
Extract the ZIP archive and open `index.html` in any web browser.
