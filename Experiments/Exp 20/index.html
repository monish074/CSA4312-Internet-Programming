OUR HERITAGE & PHILOSOPHY

Crafting Memorable Evenings Since 2014

Founded inside a restored 1920s heritage courtyard, Gourmet Haven began with a singular culinary conviction: fine dining should feel deeply warm, unhurried, and rooted in honest regional ingredients.

Our kitchen partners directly with organic hill-station farms, coastal day-boat fishermen, and artisanal cheesemakers—transforming peak-season harvests over open hearth embers and precision modern techniques.
