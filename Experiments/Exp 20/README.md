# Gourmet Haven — Contemporary Fine Dining Website

A self-contained, multi-page-style fine-dining web application featuring a rotating hero carousel, interactive category-filtered culinary menu, online table reservation system, chef & kitchen team profiles, high-resolution lightbox gallery, seasonal events, patron testimonials, and an interactive location map with contact form.

## Included Sections
1. **Homepage**: Auto-advancing rotating carousel of the restaurant's interior, exterior, and signature dishes, welcoming philosophy, and verified patron reviews.
2. **Interactive Menu**: Switch seamlessly between `Appetizers & Crudos`, `Main Courses`, `Artisanal Desserts`, and `Cellar & Botanical Elixirs` with full ingredient breakdowns and pricing.
3. **Online Table Reservation System**: Calendar date picker, evening seating slots, party size selector, celebration notes, and persistent reservation confirmation tickets.
4. **About Us**: Restaurant history, culinary philosophy, and portraits/bios of the Executive Chef, Head Pastry Chef, and Head Sommelier.
5. **Special Offers & Events**: Seasonal 7-course tasting gala, twilight lounge flights, and upcoming celebrations.
6. **High-Resolution Gallery**: Full-screen lightbox viewer showcasing the dining salon, heritage exterior, wine cellar, and signature plates.
7. **Contact & Interactive Map**: Physical address, phone, email, operating hours, embedded interactive location map widget, and concierge inquiry form.

## How to Run
Extract the ZIP archive and open `index.html` in any modern web browser.
